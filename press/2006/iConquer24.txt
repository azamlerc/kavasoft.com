KavaSoft releases iConquer 2.4, the game of world conquest for Mac OS X

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

July 5, 2006 - KavaSoft has announced iConquer 2.4, a new version of the game of world conquest for Mac OS X. This version features a new graphs window, an integrated guided tour, and improved compatibility with the upcoming Mac OS X 10.5 Leopard. 

The graphs window provides real-time graphs of all game statistics, allowing you to monitor your world dominance over time. Graphs display the number of countries, armies or cards owned by each player, their income, and their victories and defeats. The guided tour allows new users to learn quickly about the features of iConquer. 

iConquer allows up to 6 players to play against each other over the Internet. iConquer can also be played against electronic opponents, each with a distinct personality managed by the game's artificial intelligence engine.

iConquer can be played on over twenty different maps, including several high-resolution satellite world maps, the states of the United States, the countries of Europe, the cantons of Switzerland, and even the Solar System. Users can also create their own maps by downloading the free iConquer Plug-in Kit from the KavaSoft website.

iConquer costs $25, and comes with a free 10-game trial. You can purchase iConquer using PayPal or Kagi. iConquer is available in English, German, French, Japanese, Italian and Dutch.
