KavaSoft announces KavaMovies 1.1, the Mac movie database

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

December 1, 2008 - KavaSoft today announced KavaMovies 1.1, the Mac movie database. KavaMovies lets you organize the movies you've seen, the movies you want to see, and the movies in your collection. It connects to the internet and downloads tons of information about each of your movies in realtime. Version 1.1 lets you discover new movies by clicking on charts, keywords and directors. 

Imagine making a list of all the movies you've ever seen? You'd be surprised how easy it is with KavaMovies. Click a movie to see who's in it. Click an actor to see what they've been in. Directors, genres, keywords, languages, years… everything is connected!

Use KavaMovies to make a list of all the movies you want to see. Click on a movie you like, and it will recommend ten other movies. Better yet, the program makes a master list all the movies you're most likely to enjoy, based on all the movies in your personal database.

KavaMovies 1.1 adds charts that let you discover new movies easier than ever before. The top 250 movies, this weekend's box office results, video rentals, and top lists by decade and genre are just a click away. Charts are updated in realtime, so you'll always have the latest movies.

New in KavaMovies 1.1, keywords are now linked to related movies. Click on cult favorite, coming of age, neo noir, romance, or whatever you're in the mood for this evening to find more great movies. 

KavaMovies organizes all the movies on your computer (or your shelf). Drag a movie file to the program and it remembers where it is. Just click play to watch whatever you want. It's your whole collection at your fingertips. 

Don't have a movie? Toolbar buttons let you buy it from Amazon on DVD, or from the iTunes Store online. And downloading with BitTorrent has never been simpler: one click to search, another click to start the download in your favorite client.

KavaMovies costs $35, and can be purchased using PayPal or Kagi. You can try all the features of KavaMovies with up to fifty movies for free. The KavaSoft Store is offering a limited-time $10 introductory discount with coupon code "MOVIEFAN". 

Organize your movies, music and photos! KavaMovies is now available as part of the KavaLife suite, along with KavaTunes and Shoebox Express for only $80.
